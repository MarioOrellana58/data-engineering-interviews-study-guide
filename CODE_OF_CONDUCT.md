# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We want this project to be a welcoming place for people learning, teaching, reviewing, and contributing across different backgrounds and experience levels.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, specific, and constructive.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Assume good intent while accepting corrections.
- Give credit for others' work and disclose relevant sources.
- Protect private, confidential, and identifying information.
- Make room for questions without mocking a person's experience or language ability.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, discrimination, stalking, or sexualized attention.
- Personal attacks, insults, trolling, or sustained disruption.
- Publishing another person's private information without permission.
- Sharing confidential interview material, credentials, or employer-owned content.
- Retaliating against someone who reports a concern in good faith.

## Scope and enforcement

This code applies in repository discussions, issues, pull requests, reviews, and other spaces where someone represents this project.

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through their GitHub profile. Do not open a public issue containing sensitive details. Maintainers may edit or remove content and may warn, temporarily restrict, or permanently ban participants when necessary. Reports will be handled as confidentially as reasonably possible, and conflicts of interest should be disclosed.

If there is an immediate threat or illegal activity, contact the appropriate local authority or platform safety team.
