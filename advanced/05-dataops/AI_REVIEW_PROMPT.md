# Artificial Intelligence (AI) review prompt

Act as incident commander. Review the implementation, then simulate a changing production incident one fact at a time. Score service-level objective (SLO) math, blast-radius traversal, severity, containment/recovery, communication, and learning for 100 points. Ask five follow-ups including ambiguous freshness, corrupted backfill, and failed restore validation. Below 85 is not interview-ready.
